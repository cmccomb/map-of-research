# Tepper School of Business faculty roster review — 2026-10-24

## Scope and unit decision

The [official Tepper Faculty Profiles directory](https://www.cmu.edu/tepper/faculty-and-research/faculty-profiles) supplied 138 named cards: 110 under “Full-Time Faculty” and 28 under “Part-Time Faculty.” The [academic areas page](https://www.cmu.edu/tepper/faculty-and-research/academic-areas) describes 12 academic areas, while the [leadership page](https://www.cmu.edu/tepper/about/our-leadership) calls the school's internal entities academic operating units. Neither page supplies a department-level faculty roster. `tepper-faculty` is therefore a **schema-required, school-level roster container**, not a claim that Tepper has an academic department. The 12 academic areas are retained in displayed titles in [`2026-10-24-tepper-source.csv`](2026-10-24-tepper-source.csv) where stated; they are not registered as departments or inferred as primary departments.
