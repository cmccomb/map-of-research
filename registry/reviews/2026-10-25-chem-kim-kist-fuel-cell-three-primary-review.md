The full-name attribution below is an inference from unusually specific,
contemporary original and institutional records; it does not assume that
other initials-only fuel-cell papers share the same author.

from 2004 through 2022. CMU's [faculty biography](https://www.cmu.edu/chemistry/people/faculty/kim.html)
identifies **Hyung J. Kim** as the chemistry professor at CMU during those
years. These sources distinguish two people despite the shared initials.
