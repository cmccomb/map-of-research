# College of Fine Arts faculty directory review — 2026-10-24

The [CFA schools page](https://cfa.cmu.edu/schools-and-academic-programs)
identifies five internal schools: Architecture, Art, Design, Drama, and Music.
Those five schools are the five academic units in this registry addition. BXA,
CFA Photography, and Arts & Entertainment Management are programs rather than
additional schools on that page; this pass does not invent departments for
them. [The dated source extract](2026-10-24-cfa-source.csv) preserves every
displayed directory name, title, category, role decision, profile link,
directly linked Scholar ID, and source-backed primary affiliation decision.

These are directory observations, not an independently audited headcount. The
367 memberships represent 359 unique people, and the 256 included
memberships represent 256 unique people in this CFA slice. The people fragment
has 348 new identities and one existing-person provenance update. Eight
people appear in more than one CFA school; one ID is reused for each. Existing
Engineering/SCS IDs were also reused for cross appointments. The Music page
prints “122 bios displayed,” although its alphabetical directory contains 121
named cards in the retrieved page. That one-entry difference is an open source
gap, not a fabricated faculty row.

The registered role boundary follows the title and labeled directory section.
Professor, teaching professor, and emeritus appointments are included;
adjunct, affiliate, courtesy, special, and visiting roles are retained as
excluded memberships. Pure staff and Design PhD teaching fellows remain in
the source extract only. Art's two visiting professors remain excluded despite
appearing under “Full-Time Faculty.” The School of Music uses “Artist Lecturer”
as a teaching appointment: its [faculty announcement](https://www.cmu.edu/cfa/music/news/2025/bell-harguindey-join-faculty.html)
describes artist lecturers as faculty teaching studios, and the
[current course catalog](https://coursecatalog.web.cmu.edu/schools-colleges/collegeoffinearts/schoolofmusic/)
places them in its faculty section. The catalog also supports the teaching
appointments of Seul Lee, Mark Snyder, and Stephen Story where the directory
card prints a functional title. [Richard Stern's Music bio](https://www.cmu.edu/cfa/music/people/Bios/stern_richard.html)
calls him an Artist Lecturer in Music and an ECE professor, so his Music
teaching membership is included while ECE remains the display primary.
[Thomas Sullivan's Music bio](https://www.cmu.edu/cfa/music/people/Bios/sullivan_thomas.html)
explicitly calls his Music lectureship courtesy, so that membership is
excluded and ECE remains primary.

The Architecture and Design pages are mixed-category directories. For example,
Architecture's [Burcu Akinci profile](https://www.architecture.cmu.edu/profiles/burcu-akinci)
is an affiliated listing with her College of Engineering professorship, and
Design labels [Golan Levin](https://www.design.cmu.edu/faculty) a courtesy
appointment while Art lists him as a full-time professor. Their school
memberships retain those different roles under one identity. Design lists
Andrew Twigg under Faculty with only an administrative title; the
[current CMU course catalog](https://coursecatalog.web.cmu.edu/schools-colleges/collegeoffinearts/schoolofdesign/)
gives his Associate Teaching Professor rank. Design's Derek Ham and Drama's
Robert Ramirez remain included from the explicit “Faculty” sections although
their cards print director/head titles rather than a rank. Drama's David
Holcomb is also listed under Staff, without a faculty rank, so his repeated
cards remain source-only.

Four included CFA profiles directly link Google Scholar IDs: Daragh Byrne,
Yiwei Lyu, Azadeh O. Sawyer, and Dina El-Zanfaly. Their official school
profiles support Architecture or Design as the display primary; the extract
records each profile URL and date. Architecture also directly links Robert
Heard's previously registered MSE Scholar ID, and his MSE teaching title
supports MSE as primary. The 13 known-ID people in this CFA slice each have a
source-backed primary in [`2026-10-24-other-schools-primary.csv`](2026-10-24-other-schools-primary.csv); one pre-existing multi-department
choice for Destenie Nock is inherited from draft PR #35 and is subject to the
campus-wide advising-evidence review. Direct profile links were read, but no
Google Scholar page or publication was requested.

The current registry has no primary-department columns. [`2026-10-24-other-schools-primary.csv`](2026-10-24-other-schools-primary.csv) is a
schema-aligned ledger with PR #35's three primary fields plus a decision basis;
it does not discard any memberships. New single-department people can derive
their primary under PR #35, while the directly linked ID cases have the
explicit profile evidence in the extract. All five schools need annual
directory re-review. The directory count discrepancy in Music and functional
titles without explicit rank are the main source limitations in this slice.
