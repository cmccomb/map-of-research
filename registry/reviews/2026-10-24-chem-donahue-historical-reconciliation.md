## Source rule and next schema step

The current `curated_verified` loader and snapshot validator require a DOI. The four conference records above have verified venue identifiers or program locations, but no verified DOI for the specific historical items. Do not invent a DOI, assign a current Scholar ID, or claim the 2026 profile displays them. A follow-on schema change can accept a stable source-native identifier plus its authoritative URL, for example `EGU2012:EGU2012-2516-1`, `EGU2009:EGU2009-9565`, `AGU2009:A21H-08`, and the A&WMA 2007 proceedings title/page. It should form a distinct curated observation key, validate the source URL and verification date, preserve `source_type=curated_verified`, and reconcile later Scholar rows by exact title/year or a later verified DOI. The old Scholar IDs belong in this historical ledger as provenance, not in newly synthesized current Scholar observations. Review the A&WMA full paper and any source-native item record before promoting its proceedings entry.
