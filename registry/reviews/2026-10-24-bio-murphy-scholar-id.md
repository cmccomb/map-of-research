His [Bio individual profile](https://www.cmu.edu/bio/people/faculty/murphy.html) names Robert F. Murphy, labels his photo “Bob Murphy,” and directly links Google Scholar ID `qQLlBH4AAAAJ`. Murphy as professor emeritus. His [CMU-hosted personal homepage](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/murphy/) confirms the same Scholar profile.

The Bio directory's Bob Murphy and Computational Biology's Robert F. Murphy were registered as two people. This change attaches the Bio emeritus membership to the existing Robert F. Murphy person, retains `Bob Murphy` as that membership's source label, and removes the now-orphaned Bob Murphy person. The existing Robert F. Murphy record already owns this Scholar ID; this change adds no new global Scholar ID, publication row, or claim that his complete Scholar history has been captured.
