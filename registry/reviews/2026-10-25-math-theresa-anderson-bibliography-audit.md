# Theresa Anderson bibliography source audit (review only)

**Decision:** Preserve her complete displayed author bibliography and CV crosscheck, but do not publish it as Google Scholar data. No exact Scholar account is verified, and publication metadata and genre still need review.

[CMU identifies Anderson](https://www.cmu.edu/math/people/faculty/anderson.html) as Mathematical Sciences faculty. Her [CMU hosted research page](https://faculty.math.cmu.edu/anderson/research/) lists 42 entries under “Publications and preprints”; its linked [August 2026 CV](https://faculty.math.cmu.edu/anderson/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/CV2026.pdf) numbers 42 entries. The [42-row website capture](2026-10-25-math-theresa-anderson-author-site-observations.csv), [source HTML](2026-10-25-math-theresa-anderson-author-page.html), and [42-pair CV crosswalk](2026-10-25-math-theresa-anderson-cv-crosswalk.csv) preserve every displayed entry and its original wording. All 42 website entries match different CV entries; the website and CV sometimes order them differently. The [manifest](2026-10-25-math-theresa-anderson-bibliography-manifest.json) pins source checksums and marks the material release ineligible.

The page spans a 2011 article through 2026 papers and current preprints. Website ordinal **37** is a PhD thesis, **11** is a personal essay in *Notices of the AMS*, and **5** is an appendix to another author's paper. These entries need distinct genre and authorship treatment; the host article's author must not become an appendix coauthor by string parsing. Student coauthor markers `*` and `**` in the CV are annotations, not characters in names.

Website ordinals **21, 22, 23, 24, 25, and 33** have title or citation differences from the CV. Ordinal 23 has three title variants across the website, CV, and [publisher article 125620](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022247X21006995), DOI `10.1016/j.jmaa.2021.125620`. Shared authors, journal, volume, issue, and article number identify one work; keep all raw titles and assign a reviewed canonical publication title later. The [NYJM article](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2018/24-1.html) confirms that ordinal 33's website title is abbreviated. The crosswalk records source wording rather than silently replacing it.

This review establishes a **complete capture of the two displayed author sources**, not independent proof that every academic work is listed. Neither official page links a Scholar account. A prior bounded Scholar identity search found no safely attributable ID. A later verified Scholar profile should be captured in full and reconciled as the preferred ground truth. Until then these rows remain review observations, not `author_site` or Scholar entries in the release snapshot. Resolve publication versions, full coauthors, title variants, and exact work overlaps before graph ingestion. This PR changes no registry identity, source snapshot, producer code, Hub dataset, or public map.
