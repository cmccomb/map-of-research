Each of the 27 people in the [review ledger](2026-10-26-scs-single-membership-primary-homes.csv) has exactly one included membership in the current registry and a recorded Scholar ID, but no explicit primary academic home. Their academic home records do not authorize opening, assigning, or publishing a Scholar profile. No Scholar profile was opened for this metadata review.
