profile](https://www.cmu.edu/math/people/faculty/iyer.html) identifies Gautam
Iyer and links his researcher site. Its current “All Publications” page has 47

Mathematics Colloquium Bulletin* (1998), and mark it as an undergraduate
publication. The two CMU-hosted PDF byte streams are retained under
[`data/source_reviews/gautam-iyer-1998/`](../../data/source_reviews/gautam-iyer-1998/)
with SHA-256 hashes in the manifest. Their extracted text spells the exponent
as `Rn`; the PDF typography is `R^n`.

The exact title does not occur among the 47 captured entries on the current
author page. The cross-source union therefore has **48 distinct observations**:
47 page entries plus this one CV-only item. This count describes observed
entries, not 48 independently verified published works. The CVs give no
volume, issue, pages, DOI, or full-text link. A bounded primary-source search
corroborated the Bulletin's existence in 1998 but did not locate an
article-level publisher record for Iyer's item. Do not invent those fields.
