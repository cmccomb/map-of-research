Two initially flagged author-display omissions are **valid Kinderlehrer publications** and stay eligible. The [journal's complete author list](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/dcds.2011.30.427) includes Kinderlehrer for row 63, *An entropy based theory of the grain boundary character distribution*. The [conference publisher's author list](https://www.scientific.net/MSF.715-716.473) includes him for row 79, *Grain Growth and the Puzzle of its Stagnation in Thin Films: A Detailed Comparison of Experiments and Simulations*. Scholar's displayed author strings are incomplete on those rows.

The two *How a minimal surface leaves an obstacle* rows are distinct publications despite their shared title. Row 115 is the 1972 [Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society note](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9904-1972-13071-4), pp. 969–971. Row 52 is the 1973 [Acta Mathematica article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02392266), pp. 221–242. Both Crossref DOI records name Kinderlehrer, identify different journals and page spans, and remain eligible.

The two *A theory and challenges for coarsening in microstructure* rows remain unresolved as an exact-record pair. [CMU's seven-author working-paper record](https://www.math.cmu.edu/CNA/Publications/publication_auto.php?cnacode=12-CNA-013) and the [Springer chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-88-470-2592-9_12) (2013, pp. 193–220, DOI `10.1007/978-88-470-2592-9_12`) support the complete authorship represented by row 111. Row 141 has only `D Kinderlehrer`, no year or venue. A [2013 BIRS abstract book](https://archive.birs.ca/files/2013/13w5004/Abstracts13w5004.pdf) and a [separate conference session abstract](https://cdn.eventsforce.net/files/ef-q5vmtsq56tk6/website/484/session_j4_mathematical_modeling_and_analysis.pdf) contain solo Kinderlehrer abstracts with the same title. The available Scholar row cannot be tied conclusively to either a distinct abstract or a duplicate of the chapter. Keep row 141 eligible in the raw and normalized source pending exact Scholar detail or venue evidence.
