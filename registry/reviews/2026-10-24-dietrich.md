## Validation and limits

Before the final cross-school merge, a temporary Dietrich-only upsert over `origin/main` passed `map_of_research.registry.load_registry`: 1,307 people, 1,562 memberships, 31 units, four top-level schools. The final combined registry is validated separately in this PR. All nine unit directory entrypoints returned HTTP 200, including the general History and LCAL people pages. All 474 distinct reviewed profile URLs fetched successfully after percent-encoding two non-ASCII paths. This review is a dated observation of public pages, not a guarantee that each directory is complete or internally consistent. No collection, upload, or deployment was performed.
