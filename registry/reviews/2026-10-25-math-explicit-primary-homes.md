# Explicit Mathematical Sciences display homes

The [CMU Mathematical Sciences faculty directory](https://www.cmu.edu/math/people/faculty/index.html)
is the official source for the included faculty, teaching, and emeritus
memberships reviewed on 2026-10-24. The directory was rechecked on
2026-10-25. This change records `math` explicitly for the 49 included Math
people whose display home was previously inferred from their sole included
department membership. Each record retains that membership's source URL,
verification date, and `single_included_membership` basis. The basis describes
the evidence; the explicit field does not claim an institutional HR primary.

All 65 included Math people now have an explicit display home. The remaining
16 were already explicit, including every joint appointee. The seven joint
membership sets and their existing display choices are unchanged: Gérard P.
Cornuéjols (Tepper Operations Research), Jeremy Avigad (Philosophy), John
Mackey (Math), John P. Lehoczky (Statistics), Manuel Blum (Computer Science),
Nicholas Boffi (Machine Learning), and Robert F. Sekerka (Physics).

The frozen implicit Scholar-primary baseline remains intact. This backfill
must not change any published `primary_academic_home_*` or
`primary_department_*` value: the recorded source, date, ID, type, and basis
match the values already produced by the single-membership rule. It changes
registry storage only; it does not add Scholar IDs, observations, works, or
map links.
