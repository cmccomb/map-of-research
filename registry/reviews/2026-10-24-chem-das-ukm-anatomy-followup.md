# Das attribution follow-up: UKM Anatomy records

This draft adds twelve exact false-attribution decisions for captured Scholar rows. Nine original journal PDFs place the `S. Das` author in the Department of Anatomy at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM), and three exact records appear in UKM Anatomy's own annual publication lists. [UKM identifies Srijit Das as its former Anatomy professor](https://www.ukm.my/anatomifper/ms/profil-penyelidik-test/), while [CMU identifies Subha R. Das as its Chemistry faculty member](https://www.cmu.edu/chemistry/people/faculty/das.html). The row-level evidence supports exclusion from the CMU profile even where a journal printed only initials.
