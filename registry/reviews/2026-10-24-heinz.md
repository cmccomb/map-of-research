## Units and assignment policy

[Heinz history](https://www.heinz.cmu.edu/about/history) states that the college houses two graduate schools, the **School of Information Systems and Management** and the **School of Public Policy and Management**, and that Heinz is not organized into academic departments. The current [programs page](https://www.heinz.cmu.edu/programs) documents both school structures and is the strongest current school-specific page found. It is recorded as each school's `directory_url` because the registry requires that field; it is **not a faculty directory**. No distinct faculty roster for either school was found.

The [faculty finder](https://www.heinz.cmu.edu/faculty-research/profiles/) is college-wide. `heinz-faculty` is a technical roster container under school `heinz`, not a third academic school or department. All 273 nonstudent entries have one membership there. The two actual schools also have rows in `departments.csv` to fit the current schema. The nine school-level memberships come from individual profile language, not from program names or a presumed split of the shared finder:

## Remaining source gaps

- The shared finder supplies no complete ISM or PPM roster. The `heinz-faculty` primary for Pedro is therefore a college-level display placement, and the 77 other included people without explicit school language have no school-specific assignment.
- Heinz profile links exposed one direct Scholar ID, which belongs to an already canonical person; all newly authored Heinz people require later independent, official-linked ID evidence before collection.
- Some directory profiles contain older career descriptions. They are treated as observed on the current official directory, while adjunct/affiliate-only roles remain excluded unless a current official appointment page overrides the filter.
- Chris Hendrickson's title-based CEE choice is a display inference, not a claim that the CEE/EPP/Heinz doctoral advising split or university HR primary is known.
