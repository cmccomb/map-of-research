# Neil Donahue Scholar capture (staged for source review, 2026-10-24)

[CMU Chemistry](https://www.cmu.edu/chemistry/people/faculty/donahue.html) identifies Neil M. Donahue as a professor of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering and links Google Scholar profile `ILambBMAAAAJ`. The direct profile opened in a headed Chrome session. Six **Show more** clicks exposed 623 rows; the button was then disabled. No visible Scholar page error or access challenge appeared. The named Chrome session was closed.

The saved HTML, the browser's original text extraction, and a corrected SVG-aware JSON extraction are under `data/captures/donahue-visible-623-2026-10-24.*`. Two hidden Scholar `xsrf` input values were redacted in the staged HTML; the untouched capture stays in local scratch, and its SHA-256 is recorded in the receipt. No publication or SVG content changed. Scholar renders seven title symbols as SVG elements whose `aria-label` carries the visible text. The initial `textContent` extraction dropped those symbols, including `α` in “α-pinene” and `x` in “NOx.” The corrected JSON inserts the seven labels in document order from the saved HTML, and the separately saved original extraction makes that correction auditable. Offline validation matched all 623 rows and unique citation IDs, author strings, venues, years, displayed citation counts, continuation state, and absence of a visible page error. The corrected candidate cache is `data/captures/donahue-visible-623-2026-10-24.candidate-cache.json`; it is **outside `data/authors/` and is not ingested**. The receipt at `status/browser-capture-chem-donahue-2026-10-24.json` records each staged file's SHA-256.
