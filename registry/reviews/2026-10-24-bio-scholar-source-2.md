This review covers **all 686 visible Google Scholar rows** in six complete direct-profile captures, not a first page or a fixed first 100. The paired captures and normalized caches are staged separately; this review PR does not publish a snapshot. These are complete *visible profiles on 2026-10-24*, not a claim that Scholar contains every work ever produced or will stay unchanged.

## Source decisions

Barth's 2015 `SYSTEMS/CIRCUITS` row is a [journal section label](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/35/50/16450.full.pdf), and a concatenated 2024 title duplicates a [complete paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11261211/) on the same Scholar profile. An `AHTHOR INDEX` row is a volume author index. Two title matches are [Tokyo](https://www.neurochem.m.u-tokyo.ac.jp/english/neurochemistry_seminar.html) [seminar notices](https://www2.kansai-u.ac.jp/cee/research/data/seminar20170317tokyo.pdf), not publications. The [published author list](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22542246/) for *Sensitive time-windows for susceptibility in neurodevelopmental disorders* does not include Barth, despite the Scholar profile's attribution. The review excludes those six citation IDs and keeps her real papers.

Yttri's *Open Science Symposium 2018 Program* is an event program; [CMU Libraries identifies the symposium series](https://events.library.cmu.edu/oss2019/). His yearless *CMU Array* row is retained: a [BRAIN Initiative project source](https://www.braininitiative.org/toolmakers/resources/3d-nanoparticle-printing-for-customized-ultra-high-density-multielectrode-arrays/) identifies a [2019 bioRxiv version](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/742346v1), and the profile also shows another preprint version and a later paper. The review does not collapse versions by title alone. Hovis's sixth row is [volume contents for *Journal of Neuroscience Methods*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-neuroscience-methods/vol/191/issue/2), not a research paper; the other five source rows remain.

Zhang's *The nuclear bodies conference* row belongs to [Kaufman, Huang, and Pederson](https://faseb.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1096/fj.202201455R), with no Zhang in the publisher author list. Her yearless *Come get dissolved in PML bodies* is retained: [Nature Chemical Biology DOI 10.1038/s41589-025-01960-x](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41589-025-01960-x) identifies a genuine 2025 Zhang work. That DOI is a verified candidate for a labeled `curated_verified` fallback if the ingest pipeline needs to fill the blank year later; this review does not overwrite the Scholar row. Two *Chemical dimerization-induced protein condensates* observations are retained because JoVE has [article DOI 10.3791/62173](https://www.jove.com/t/62173/chemical-dimerization-induced-protein-condensates-on-telomeres) and [video DOI 10.3791/62173-v](https://www.jove.com/v/62173/chemical-dimerization-induced-protein-condensates-on-telomeres).

Zhao's 86 rows use the corrected active CMU-linked Scholar ID `0-Bv_qMAAAAJ`; an older candidate ID returned 404 and was not combined. No exact exclusion is supported in this pass. Same-title *Optogenetic probes* observations correspond to [different US patent numbers](https://cohenweb.rc.fas.harvard.edu/Publications/publications.html), so a title-only deduplication would erase source history.

The registry keeps all verified memberships. Barth and Yttri are included in Biological Sciences and have courtesy Biomedical Engineering memberships; Hovis is a Biological Sciences teaching faculty member; Zhang has Biological Sciences plus courtesy Physics. Zhao has both Biological Sciences and Biomedical Engineering included memberships. His [CMU Biological Sciences profile](https://www.cmu.edu/bio/people/faculty/zhao.html) lists a Biological Sciences mailing address, while [CMU Biomedical Engineering](https://www.cmu.edu/bme/People/Faculty/profile/yzhao.html) lists his joint role. Murphy has included Biological Sciences and Computational Biology memberships. The [CMU CBD profile](https://www.cmu.edu/cbd/people/murphy.html) names him Ray and Stephanie Lane Professor of Computational Biology Emeritus; the [CMU Bio page](https://www.cmu.edu/bio/people/faculty/murphy.html) also lists him. These sources support review of an identified primary department while preserving the other membership. This PR records the evidence and does not change primary-department assignments; the separate primary-affiliation work owns those decisions.

Unresolved or potentially distinct records remain visible for later review, including Murphy's genuine book review, some older abbreviated-title items, and multiple preprint, patent, or video versions on other profiles. Exact exclusion is intentionally narrower than automated title matching. Publication counts in the table are raw Scholar rows, not the number of works or collaboration links that a later map snapshot will produce.
