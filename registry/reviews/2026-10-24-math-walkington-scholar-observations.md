# Walkington Scholar observation review

[Draft source PR #104](https://github.com/cmccomb/map-of-research/pull/104) preserves all **144** rows visible on Noel Walkington's [named Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=CGSMDGwAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate) after `Show more` disabled. Chris McComb confirmed that direct profile looks like Walkington in this chat on 2026-10-24. Scholar still displayed `Unknown affiliation` and `No verified email`; [draft registry PR #108](https://github.com/cmccomb/map-of-research/pull/108) records that caveat.

Eight rows were flagged because the displayed author string omitted Walkington or the record appeared to represent a container. Two exact rows are excluded from downstream works and coauthor links; raw HTML, JSON, and cache are unchanged.
