Title: On the Critical Behavior of the Length of Shortest Paths in Regular and Invasion Percolation
Author(s): Agoston Pisztora*+
Affiliation(s): Carnegie Mellon University
Address: , , , ,
Keywords: critical exponent ; percolation ; shortest path
Abstract: In supercritical percolation, the length of a shortest path between two far away sites in the infinite cluster is proportional the their Euclidean distance. The proportionality factor, called the tortuosity coefficient, explodes as p approaches the critical point. In this talk some arguments will be presented indicating a power-law behavior of the tortuosity coefficient along with an inequality relating the corresponding (fictive) exponent to other (more standard) critical exponents. As a (not quite straightforward) application, the power-law behavior of shortest paths in invasion percolation will be discussed.
