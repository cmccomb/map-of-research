in Mathematical Sciences and links his [researcher-owned page](https://www.math.cmu.edu/~clintonc/). The visible **Papers, preprints, and notes** section has 31 entries. This directory preserves the original HTML and the full 31-row transcription, including each title, coauthor clause, and work link. Seven links go to arXiv; their seven primary API records are pinned here. The other 24 links point to author-hosted PDFs. Their first pages were checked for title and byline, and `pdf-first-page-review.csv` records the URL, source PDF SHA-256, extracted-first-page SHA-256, and reviewed genre or version hold. The PDFs themselves are not duplicated in this repository.

Two further titles occur **only in an HTML comment**, outside the visible list. One points to the same `matching.pdf` as visible row 13. They are not counted as visible source entries or additional works.

Both year facts are retained, with no chosen display year yet.

The [Cambridge publisher page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-symbolic-logic/article/abs/vladimir-kanovei-marcin-sabok-and-jindrich-zapletal-canonical-ramsey-theory-on-polish-spaces-cambridge-tracts-in-mathematics-vol-202-cambridge-university-press-cambridge-2013-viii-269-pp/A21A98FA048B33F5B106B2CF2F355A86) labels it “Reviews” and gives his CMU Mathematical Sciences email.
