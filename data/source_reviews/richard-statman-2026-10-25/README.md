# Richard Statman: held CMU publication-source review (2026-10-25)

**Status:** source review only. This directory adds no Scholar ID, snapshot observation, work, authorship, Hub row, or graph node. The 37 attributed source observations below are not 37 verified independent works or a lifetime publication count.

## Identity and bounded sources

[CMU Mathematical Sciences](https://www.cmu.edu/math/people/faculty/statman.html) identifies Richard Statman as a professor, with `rs31@andrew.cmu.edu`, and prints **12 Select Publications** dated 2013–2018. The [CMU Math sample faculty publication PDF](https://www.cmu.edu/math/research/docs/sample-faculty-publications-2018-2023.pdf) says he had **nine papers since 2018**, but prints only **four Selected Recent Publications**. These 16 rows were already transcribed in the local 2026-10-25 source review and are preserved verbatim in `cmu-selected-16.csv`, along with the raw HTML, PDF, and extracted PDF text.

A public [CMU KiltHub/Figshare article search](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/search) for `Richard Statman`, page size 100, returned 19 hits on page 1 and none on page 2. Every search hit and detail response is preserved, including two theses by **William J. Gunther** and **Matthew P. Szudzik** that mention Statman but do not list him as author. The other 17 records all carry KiltHub author ID `5364608`, displayed as `Richard  Statman`. Their downloadable covers name **Rick Statman** at Carnegie Mellon Mathematics/Mathematical Sciences. The institutional repository links and author/cover evidence together support attribution to the CMU faculty member; the two theses are explicitly excluded in `kilthub-search-19.csv`.

The repository calls all 17 attributed items “journal contribution,” but 15 covers supply CMU Mathematics research-report numbers from 1988–1998. The other two are a 1992 CMU manuscript (*Simply typed λ calculus with surjective pairing*) and an April 1996 manuscript (*On Cartesian monoids*); KiltHub describes both as “Mathematics Technical Report.” `kilthub-search-19.csv` preserves the repository type, description, byline, cover report number when present, deposit DOI, and SHA-256 of each downloaded PDF. The 13.8 MB of downloaded PDFs remain in local scratch rather than this PR; the raw API detail JSON and public download links are committed. A KiltHub DOI identifies a **repository deposit**, not automatically a separately published journal paper.

`source-observations-37.csv` crosswalks all 12 profile, four selected-recent, 17 attributed repository, one publisher-confirmed conference paper, one publisher-confirmed book, one coauthor-listed historical report, and one arXiv preprint observation. It keeps every observation held. `manifest.json` pins the 35 committed source/data files by SHA-256. These bounded sources make no complete-career claim. No exact attributable Google Scholar profile URL was found in the official links or bounded account search, so `person-richardstatman-2e46bf59` retains a blank Scholar ID.

Dowek's [own bibliography](https://lsv.ens-paris-saclay.fr/~dowek/papers.html) lists the same title and byline as CMU technical report `CMU-CS-92-152` in **1992**. They are held as a possible single work family, never silently counted as two independent publications or released as an edge.

## Broader bibliography discovery

[CsAuthors' Richard Statman page](https://www.csauthors.net/richard-statman/) links to his CMU page and displays **67** bibliography entries dated 1977–2023. Its own source note says most records come from DBLP and that its dataset can omit papers. `csauthors-page.html.gz` pins the complete displayed page; `csauthors-67.json` preserves each listed title, byline, venue text, type, link, source record key, and row hash. An exact normalized-title comparison finds **18** entries matching one or more of the 37 already held source observations and **49** without such a title match. Title matching does not establish publication identity or prove 49 additional works: spelling and version variants occur in this bibliography. Every entry is labeled `third_party_bibliography_discovery` with release, map, and collaboration eligibility false. These 67 candidates are **separate from** the 37 attributed primary/institutional source observations and require primary-source crosswalk before graph use.

`csauthors-crossref-38.json` pins publisher-deposited Crossref metadata for **38** of the 49 unmatched title leads that expose DOI-style links. All 38 DOIs resolve and each deposited byline includes exactly one Statman. **27** deposited titles match the secondary title after basic normalization; **11** differ in wording or mathematical notation and need version/title review. Six registrations do not expose a `published` date, so the secondary year was not substituted as a publisher date. All 38 remain individually marked as publisher-registration candidates with release, map, and collaboration eligibility false; the bundle is not an assertion of 38 newly distinct works or a complete bibliography.
