# Manuel Blum: historical bibliography source hold

This review pins all 59 numbered citations in the *Publications* section of
[Manuel Blum's CMU-hosted CV](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~mblum/pdfs/manuel_blum_cv.pdf).
The CSV preserves each original citation, ordinal, source URL, and
source PDF checksum. The CV was last modified in 2011 and its final numbered
entry is from 2006. The raw PDF is not committed because the bibliography is
the relevant evidence and the CV also contains personal contact information.

A [2020 PNAS paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32291338/) names
Blum with his CMU School of Computer Science affiliation and email and is
absent from the 59-item CV list. This establishes a concrete completeness gap.
The numbered citations also contain reports, talks, preliminary versions, and
journal articles that have not been resolved into distinct academic works.

`post-2011-candidates.csv` adds eleven later arXiv source observations and
one CMU-hosted 2012 draft manuscript from a bounded primary-source check.
Several are coauthored with Lenore Blum; the
2017 password preprint and the already held 2020 PNAS paper are the same
publication family in separate preprint and journal manifestations: both name
Blum and Santosh Vempala, use near-identical titles, and describe the same
human-computation model and password application. The
[arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.01204) and
[indexed PNAS article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32291338/) remain
separate source observations. The
[2021 consciousness preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.13704) explicitly
lists DOI `10.1073/pnas.2115934119` as a related PNAS publication, so those
two manifestations form another reviewed family. Its stated text overlap
with arXiv:2011.09850 does not establish that the 2020 preprint is the same
work. Other 2020–2024 consciousness records retain version and genre holds.
The 2019 password preprint now has an exact-person link: coauthor Elan
Rosenfeld's [CMU-hosted CV](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~elan/docs/Elan_CV.pdf)
lists the same title, Rosenfeld–Vempala–Blum byline, and a 2016 senior
thesis; a [CMU research abstract](https://www.cmu.edu/uro/MoM/2016%20MoM%20Abstract%20Book.pdf)
names Manuel Blum as advisor. The 2016 thesis and 2019 arXiv text still need
comparison before a work-family decision. Three older 2013–2014 password
preprints name Blum with Jeremiah Blocki; Blocki's
[CMU-hosted research page](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~jblocki/) confirms the

[GOTCHA arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1137) explicitly relates ACM
DOI `10.1145/2517312.2517319`. A
[2012 CMU-hosted manuscript](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~jblocki/Anne_Wildenhain_2012.htm)
names Anne Wildenhain, Jeremiah Blocki, Anupam Datta, and Manuel Blum; its
drafting notes leave publication genre and later version unresolved. These
are source observations, **not** twelve distinct new works or a
whole-career census. A broad “Blum, M” search also returns other researchers;
same-name results are not automatically attributed to CMU Manuel Blum. The
[broad arXiv search](https://arxiv.org/search/cs?query=Blum%2C+M&searchtype=author)
